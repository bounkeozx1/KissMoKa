What's Inside KissMoKa?

Every ingredient is chosen for a reason. Here's what makes our foundation nourishing, long-lasting, and skin-safe.

💧

Hyaluronic Acid

Hydration

Holds 1000x its weight in water, drawing moisture into the skin for all-day plumpness and comfort. Prevents foundation from settling into fine lines.

✓ Dermatologist-approved

☀️

Broad-Spectrum SPF 30

Sun Protection

Shields skin from both UVA (aging) and UVB (burning) rays. Ideal for daily wear in Thailand's sunny climate without a separate sunscreen step.

✓ Photostable formula

🌿

Vitamin E (Tocopherol)

Antioxidant

A powerful antioxidant that protects skin from free-radical damage, reduces oxidation in the formula, and adds a subtle skin-conditioning effect.

✓ Non-irritating for sensitive skin

💦

Glycerin

Moisturizer

A natural humectant that attracts moisture to skin's surface. Works synergistically with hyaluronic acid for layered, lasting hydration that won't feel heavy.

✓ Safe for all skin types

🌸

Niacinamide (Vitamin B3)

Brightening

Minimizes the appearance of pores, evens skin tone, and reduces redness. Helps foundation apply more smoothly and look more natural on skin.

✓ Clinically tested

🍑

Jojoba Seed Oil

Skin-Softening

Structurally similar to skin's natural sebum, it absorbs without clogging pores. Adds a natural, dewy glow and helps foundation move smoothly across skin.

✓ Non-comedogenic

🛡️

Titanium Dioxide

Coverage + SPF

A mineral pigment that provides gentle, physical sun protection while contributing to foundation's smooth, buildable coverage without irritating sensitive skin.

✓ Gentle mineral formula

🌊

Aloe Vera Extract

Soothing

Calms and soothes sensitive or reactive skin. Reduces potential irritation and redness, making KissMoKa comfortable for even the most delicate complexions.

✓ Safe for sensitive skin
